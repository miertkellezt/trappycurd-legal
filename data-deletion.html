Delete your data - Trappy Curd

You can remove the data Trappy Curd holds about you in several ways, from quickest to most complete.

1. Delete everything, in the game (recommended)

  1. Open the game and tap the gear icon (Settings).
  2. Tap Delete my data (bottom right) and confirm Delete everything. You need an internet connection.

This permanently deletes your online account, your cloud save, your server records (gem balance, purchased skins, reward claims, score runs and device-check result), the Facebook link and the Facebook ID stored for the friends feature, then resets the game on your device. It cannot be undone. Your best score on the global leaderboard may remain, but with no name and no account attached to it; to have that row removed too, email us (section 4). A small local marker that the one-time Facebook bonus was already claimed stays on your device so the bonus can't be claimed again; it contains no personal data.

2. Only disconnect Facebook

In Settings, in the Facebook row, tap Disconnect. This removes the Facebook link and the stored Facebook ID. Your game progress stays.

3. Remove the game from Facebook

In Facebook go to Settings & privacy > Settings > Apps and websites, find Trappy Curd and choose Remove. Facebook stops sharing data with the game. (Use section 1 or 2 first if you also want the game-side data deleted.)

4. Ask us to delete everything

Email matecsabaillesev@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data". Include:

  • the display name shown on the leaderboard and roughly when you played, and
  • the Facebook name you connected with, if any.

We delete your cloud-save progress, server records, leaderboard entries and the Facebook link within 30 days and confirm by email. Purchases remain with Google Play / the Apple App Store, and Meta, Unity and the ad network keep their own records under their own policies.
